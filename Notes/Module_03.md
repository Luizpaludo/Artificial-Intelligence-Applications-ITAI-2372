# Module 03 – AI Use Cases in Healthcare and Agriculture

## Key Topics

- Artificial Intelligence in healthcare
- Artificial Intelligence in agriculture
- Precision farming
- Computer vision and drones
- Ethical considerations of AI

## Summary

This module explored how Artificial Intelligence is improving both healthcare and agriculture. In agriculture, AI technologies such as drones, sensors, GPS, and computer vision help farmers monitor crops, detect diseases, improve irrigation, and increase productivity while reducing the use of water, fertilizers, and pesticides. AI is also being used in livestock management to count cattle and estimate their weight without causing unnecessary stress to the animals.

The module also emphasized that AI can make farming more efficient and sustainable, but it also raises important ethical concerns. Topics such as job displacement, data privacy, fairness for small farms, and transparency in AI decision-making were discussed. As AI continues to evolve, it will be important to balance technological innovation with the needs of farmers and rural communities.

## Key Takeaways

- AI helps improve efficiency in agriculture through automation and precision farming.
- Drones, sensors, GPS, and computer vision provide valuable information for crop and livestock management.
- AI can reduce costs while improving productivity and sustainability.
- Ethical issues such as privacy, transparency, fairness, and workforce changes should be considered.
- Artificial Intelligence will continue to play an important role in the future of agriculture.
