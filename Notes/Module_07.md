# Module 07 – AI in Blockchain

## Key Topics

- Artificial Intelligence in supply chain management
- Blockchain technology
- Predictive analytics
- Inventory management
- Ethical considerations of AI

## Summary

This module explored how Artificial Intelligence and blockchain technology work together to improve supply chain management and logistics. AI helps companies optimize delivery routes, predict demand, manage inventory, and analyze large amounts of operational data. Blockchain supports these AI systems by providing secure and reliable records that improve transparency and reduce the risk of fraud.

The module also discussed ethical concerns related to automation, workforce changes, and data privacy. While AI can increase efficiency and improve decision-making, organizations must ensure that employee privacy is protected and that workers have opportunities to adapt to new technologies. The combination of AI and blockchain has the potential to create smarter, more secure, and more efficient supply chains in the future.

## Key Takeaways

- AI improves supply chain operations through automation and predictive analytics.
- Blockchain provides secure and reliable data for AI systems.
- AI can optimize inventory management and transportation.
- Ethical concerns include workforce displacement and data privacy.
- AI and blockchain together can improve efficiency, transparency, and decision-making in global logistics.
