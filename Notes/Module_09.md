# Module 09 – Ethical and Societal Implications of AI

## Key Topics

- Facial recognition technology
- Artificial Intelligence in law enforcement
- Deep learning
- Privacy and data protection
- Ethical use of AI

## Summary

This module examined how facial recognition technology is used in law enforcement to identify individuals, support criminal investigations, and improve public safety. The technology uses Artificial Intelligence and deep learning to analyze facial features and compare them with images stored in databases. AI can process large amounts of visual data quickly, making investigations more efficient while assisting officers in locating suspects and missing persons.

The module also explored the ethical and societal challenges associated with facial recognition. Important topics included privacy, consent, algorithmic bias, transparency, and the possibility of incorrect identifications. While facial recognition can improve public safety, responsible implementation requires legal oversight, human review, and strong protections for individual rights.

## Key Takeaways

- Facial recognition uses AI to identify or verify individuals through facial features.
- AI can improve the speed and efficiency of criminal investigations.
- Ethical concerns include privacy, consent, bias, and transparency.
- Human oversight is necessary to reduce errors and ensure responsible use.
- Clear regulations and accountability are important for building public trust in AI technologies.
