# Module 12 – Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) in Healthcare

## Key Topics

- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- Artificial Intelligence in healthcare
- Medical decision support
- Personalized medicine
- Ethical considerations of AGI

## Summary

This module explored the future potential of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) in the healthcare industry. Unlike current AI systems that are designed for specific tasks, AGI is expected to learn, reason, and adapt across different situations in a way that is closer to human intelligence. The module discussed how AGI could improve medical diagnosis, patient care, treatment planning, and healthcare decision-making by analyzing large amounts of medical information from multiple sources.

The module also examined the challenges and ethical concerns associated with AGI. Topics such as patient privacy, data security, accountability, fairness, and the importance of human oversight were discussed. Although AGI has the potential to transform healthcare, responsible development and careful implementation will be essential before it becomes part of everyday medical practice.

## Key Takeaways

- AGI is expected to perform a wider range of tasks than today's AI systems.
- Healthcare could benefit from more accurate diagnoses and personalized treatments.
- AGI may improve efficiency by analyzing complex medical information.
- Ethical concerns include privacy, fairness, accountability, and patient safety.
- Human oversight will remain important as AI technologies continue to evolve.
