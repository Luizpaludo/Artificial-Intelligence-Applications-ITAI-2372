# Module 04 – AI in Finance and Fraud Detection

## Key Topics

- Artificial Intelligence in financial services
- Fraud detection
- Machine learning
- AI ethics
- Future of AI in finance

## Summary

This module explored how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the financial services industry. AI is widely used to detect fraudulent transactions by analyzing large amounts of financial data and identifying unusual patterns in real time. Financial institutions also use AI to process documents, summarize reports, and support decision-making, allowing employees to complete tasks more efficiently.

The module also examined ethical concerns related to AI in finance. Topics such as bias in lending decisions, transparency, and the protection of customer data were discussed. Although AI offers many advantages, human oversight remains important to ensure that financial decisions are fair, accurate, and responsible.

## Key Takeaways

- AI improves fraud detection by identifying suspicious transactions quickly.
- Machine learning helps financial institutions analyze large amounts of information.
- AI increases efficiency by automating repetitive financial tasks.
- Ethical concerns include bias, transparency, and data privacy.
- Human oversight remains essential when AI is used to support financial decisions.
