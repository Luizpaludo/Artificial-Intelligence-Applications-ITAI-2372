# Module 10 – AI in Government and Public Sector

## Key Topics

- Artificial Intelligence in government
- Disaster response
- Machine learning
- Computer vision
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## Summary

This module explored how Artificial Intelligence is improving government services, especially during natural disasters. AI systems can analyze data from satellites, drones, weather stations, sensors, and social media to help emergency management teams respond more quickly and make better decisions. Technologies such as machine learning, computer vision, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) allow governments to identify damaged areas, predict disaster impacts, and allocate emergency resources more efficiently.

The module also introduced the concept of an AI-powered citizen reporting system for smart cities. This system would allow residents to report public issues, such as potholes or damaged traffic signals, through a mobile application. AI would automatically classify each report, determine its urgency, and forward it to the appropriate government department, helping improve public services and response times.

## Key Takeaways

- AI helps governments respond more effectively during natural disasters.
- Machine learning, computer vision, and NLP improve emergency management.
- AI can process large amounts of data much faster than traditional methods.
- Smart city applications can improve communication between citizens and local governments.
- Responsible use of AI requires protecting privacy while improving public services.
