# Module 11 – AI in Manufacturing Industry

## Key Topics

- Artificial Intelligence in manufacturing
- Industrial robotics
- Automation
- Computer vision
- Workforce transition

## Summary

This module explored how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the manufacturing industry, particularly automobile production. Modern factories use industrial robots, computer vision, and AI-powered systems to automate repetitive tasks, improve product quality, and increase production efficiency. AI is also used for quality inspection, predictive maintenance, and production planning, helping manufacturers reduce errors and improve workplace safety.

The module also examined the impact of automation on employees and manufacturing communities. While AI creates new opportunities in areas such as robotics, programming, and system maintenance, it also changes workforce requirements and may reduce the need for certain traditional jobs. The discussion emphasized the importance of workforce training and helping employees develop new skills to adapt to technological advancements.

## Key Takeaways

- AI and robotics improve efficiency, quality, and safety in manufacturing.
- Computer vision helps detect product defects and supports quality control.
- Predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime and maintenance costs.
- Automation creates both opportunities and challenges for the workforce.
- Employee training and workforce development are important for adapting to new technologies.
